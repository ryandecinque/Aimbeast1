Hey! I made a small tool for Aimbeast called AimStats. Want to try it?

What it is: after each session it shows your stats in your browser: your best run per scenario as a short clip, time on target, over-aim, kills and time per kill, how long you rest between runs, and whether you're improving week to week. For some scenarios it also shows the same run played about 20% better, so you can see what to aim for. No AI, just maths on your own runs.

It's read-only: it only reads where you aim and where the bots are, and never changes scores, ranked or anything in the game. Nothing is uploaded. It all stays on your PC.

How to use it:
1. Unzip AimStats somewhere (not in the game folder). Close Aimbeast and double-click Install.bat.
2. Play Aimbeast as normal.
3. Double-click "Open AimStats.bat". Your stats open in your browser.

After a run your numbers show up within about a minute. Tick the scenarios you want videos for in the Videos box at the top.

To remove it: close Aimbeast and double-click Uninstall.bat. It puts the game back exactly as it was.

Antivirus: it might flag dwmapi.dll. That's UE4SS, the free open-source mod loader the recorder runs on (github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS).
